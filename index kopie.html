Frequently asked questions

Good to know before you start.

Ready to explore your options?

Create a profile and see both eligible and potential matches in one clear overview.

Does Acerca approve or perform the exchange?

No. Acerca identifies possible matches and helps organise the process. Any formal exchange remains subject to approval by the education authority.

Who is Acerca intended for?

The first concept focuses on interim and substitute teachers. Eligibility may differ by region, contract, specialisation and assignment.

What happens to a potential match?

You can register interest, but no transfer is initiated. With consent, anonymous data can show where current rules block useful placements.

When are my contact details shared?

Only after mutual interest and explicit consent, following the safeguards defined for the pilot.
